Privacy policy

Sudoku (سودوکو) by Fiddle Leaf Studio · Last updated November 6, 2026

This policy explains what information the Sudoku app for Android uses and why. We have kept the app simple: there are no accounts, no sign-in, and we do not run our own servers.

Information kept on your device

The app saves your settings, your puzzle in progress, your scores and runs, and the name you choose to enter. This information stays on your phone. We cannot see it, and it is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data. Entering a name is optional.

Advertising

The app is free and shows ads through Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect information such as your device's advertising ID, approximate location based on IP address, and how you interact with ads. Google uses this information under its own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. You can learn how Google uses information from apps at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), the app asks for your consent before showing personalized ads. You can reset or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings under Google, Ads.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
